How your league works

Points: PTS × 1 · REB × 1.2 · AST × 1.5 · STL × 3 · BLK × 3 · turnovers × −1. All starting spots are utility spots: any position is eligible.

The commissioner starts the draft once at least two teams join. Draft order is randomized and reverses every round. Choose a draft clock or an untimed draft. Automatic picks follow each manager’s saved queue, then alphabetical order.

Scoring starts the day after the draft. Each matchup lasts seven days. Lineups lock at midnight New York time and carry forward until changed. Roster moves become eligible the following day. Dropped players can enter 24- or 48-hour waivers. Claims use rolling priority, initially reverse draft order. Trade one active player for another, reserve injured players, and compete in optional two- or four-team playoffs. Playoff ties go to the higher seed.

Save your private recovery key. It grants control of your team; share only the invite code with friends. Scores refresh while the league is open. Configured background jobs also refresh closed leagues; job delays are caught up on the next refresh.
